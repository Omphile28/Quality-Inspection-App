Quality Inspection Management System

A modern Quality Inspection Management System developed using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Django to digitize vehicle quality inspections, inspection records, analytics, compliance documentation, and reporting processes.

Project Status - Active Development:

  The Quality Inspection Management System is currently being expanded into a full enterprise-grade Quality Assurance platform with advanced reporting, inspection workflows, analytics, and compliance management capabilities.

Project Overview:

The Quality Inspection Management System was designed to replace manual inspection paperwork with a centralized digital platform that allows Quality Inspectors to,

  - Perform vehicle quality inspections.
  - Capture inspection findings.
  - Record vehicle damage.
  - Store inspection records.
  - Monitor quality performance.
  - Generate visual analytics.
  - Access quality regulations and standards.
  - Export operational data to Excel.


Features:


_Authentication Module_

  - Employee login page
  - User registration page
  - Employee-based authentication
  - User profile information display
  - Logout functionality


_Quality Inspection Dashboard_

The dashboard acts as the central navigation hub of the application.

  Features include:
  
  - Quality Inspection
  - Inspection Records
  - Inspection Analytics
  - Quality Regulations & Standards
  - Inspector information display
  - Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices


_F2 Damage Inspection Module_

The F2 Damage Inspection form allows inspectors to perform vehicle inspections.

Captured information includes:

  - Vehicle Information
  - Vehicle VIN Number
  - Battery Percentage
  - Number of Keys
  - Number of Key Cards
  - Destination
  - Inspection Checklist
  - Door Protection Strip
  - Tow Hook & Covers
  - Interior Carpet Footprint
  - Front Passenger Seat Covers
  - Weekly Battery Identifier
  - BI Pack
  - Floor Carpets
  - Tyre Repair Kit
  - Lock Nut
  - Charging Cable G45
  - Number Plate Carrier
  - Damage Reporting
  - Damage Identification
  - Damage Comments
  - Photo Upload
  - Reject Vehicle Decision
  - Production Team Leader Approval
  - Team Leader ID Number
  - Team Leader Signature

* Inspection records are saved and displayed in spreadsheet format *


_Inspection Records Module_

Users can:

  - Filter by inspector

  - View PDF reports

  - Review historical inspection activities
